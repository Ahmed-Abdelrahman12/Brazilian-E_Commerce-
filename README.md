An end-to-end e-commerce analysis using **Microsoft Excel** to uncover sales trends, customer behavior patterns, and logistics performance from the famous Brazilian Olist Marketplace dataset (100k+ orders).

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project processes, models, and visualizes Olist's complex transactional data to provide executive-level insights regarding revenue drivers, geographic distribution, fulfillment efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Microsoft Excel**: Core analysis platform.
* **Power Query**: Data cleaning, transformation, and shaping.
* **Power Pivot**: Data modeling and building DAX measures.
* **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts**: Data aggregation and dynamic plotting.
* **Interactive Dashboards**: Dynamic visualization across multiple views.

---

## 🖥️ Dashboards & Features
The Excel workbook features interactive dashboards driven by slicers and DAX measures:

1. 📈 **Overview Dashboard**: High-level revenue performance, seasonality, and overall business health.
2. 👤 **Customer Dashboard**: Regional breakdowns, top purchase categories, and repeat behavior.
3. 🚚 **Logistics Dashboard**: Delivery SLAs, freight cost ratios, and shipping performance impact on CSAT.

---

## 🎯 Key Metrics (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue** ($)
* **Total Orders** (#)
* **Average Order Value (AOV)**
* **Average Review Score** (Out of 5)
* **Freight % of Revenue**
* **Repeat Purchase Rate** (%)

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Takeaways

| Insight Area | Key Finding | Strategic Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🚚 **Delivery SLA** | Customer review scores drop significantly when delivery time exceeds **~21 days**. | Implement SLA alerts at day 14 & expand regional fulfillment centers. |
| 📍 **Geography** | **São Paulo (SP)** generates the highest revenue and order volume. | Focus freight subsidies on high-potential secondary states (e.g., RJ, MG). |
| 📦 **Sales Pareto** | The **Top 10 product categories** account for the vast majority of orders. | Prioritize supplier inventory for top categories; optimize long-tail stock. |
| 📈 **Seasonality** | Sales consistently peak during the **mid-year period**. | Align marketing spend and logistics capacity for mid-year promo surges. |
